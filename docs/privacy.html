Plus 15 privacy policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

Plus 15 is an independent navigator for Calgary's +15 skywalk network. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by The City of Calgary. This page explains what the Android app does with your data. In short: there are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. We don't run a database of users, and we don't sell or share data for advertising.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Location. If you allow it, the app uses your location to show where you are, plan routes from "My location" and guide you along a route. It is used only while the app is open, never in the background, and it isn't stored or uploaded by the app.
  • Saved routes, saved places and settings (dark mode, step-free preference, walking pace) are stored only on your phone. Android's backup may copy them to your Google account if you have backup turned on. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Data sent to other services

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Ask +15 (only if you use it and agree first): our proxy on Cloudflare, then NVIDIA's AI service, which runs models from Moonshot AI, OpenAI and Meta Your questions and the earlier messages in that chat; roughly where you are on the +15 (for example "about 75 m from the Bankers Hall door", never GPS coordinates); your saved places; the trip open in Navigate; whether you prefer step-free routes. Your IP address is seen by Cloudflare as part of the connection. To answer your question. Our proxy keeps no logs of questions or answers. NVIDIA's handling is covered by NVIDIA's privacy policy.
Esri (map tiles); OpenStreetMap (backup map tiles) Your IP address and the map area you are viewing, as with any map website. To draw the base map.
Apps you choose to open (Google Maps, phone dialer, email, a shop's website) Whatever you send in that app. Only when you tap a link, call or directions button.

Everything is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Your choices

  • You can turn location off at any time in Android Settings. Search, the map and routes between buildings still work.
  • Ask +15 is optional. Nothing is sent to the AI service until you agree, and nothing after you stop using it. Don't include personal details in your questions.
  • Because we don't keep your data on a server, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove what's on your phone, clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
