# Plus 15: outreach drafts

These are drafts for you to send or post from your own accounts. Send them on the first cold
snap (the first −20° forecast), when "how do I stay indoors downtown" is on people's minds.
Afterwards, log what went out, and when, in `growth.md`.

Links:
- Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.plus15.plus15_navigator
- Site: https://harshalpathak97.github.io/plus15/
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xxW7FkKi67I

## 1. r/Calgary post

Check the subreddit rules first. Self-promotion may need to be a comment in a weekly thread, or a
mod may have to approve it. Post from your own account, reply to comments for the first
couple of hours, and don't cross-post the same day.

**Title:** I made a free +15 map app that gives you indoor routes bridge by bridge (no ads, no tracking)

**Body:**
> Every winter I'd end up in the wrong tower trying to get from Bankers Hall to The Bow without going outside, so I mapped the whole +15: 109 buildings, 80 bridges, 130+ shops with opening hours.
>
> You pick where you are and where you're going, and it gives you a route that names each bridge ("cross over 8 Ave SW into The CORE"). It has step-free routes, a "mostly indoors" option, and it can walk you to the nearest +15 entrance from the street.
>
> It's free on Android, with no account, no ads and no tracking. It's not affiliated with the City. iPhone isn't available yet.
>
> [Play link]
>
> I'd really like to hear about mistakes: closed bridges, wrong hours, routes that send you somewhere silly. There's a feedback link in the app, or just comment here.

**Ongoing:** search r/Calgary for "+15" and "plus 15" every week or two. Where someone is asking how
to get somewhere indoors, answer the question properly first and mention the app in one line at the end.
