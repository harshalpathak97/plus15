SHORT:
Never get lost in Calgary’s +15 again. Indoor routes, bridge by bridge.
FULL:
It’s −30°. You’re in Bankers Hall. Your meeting is in The Bow.

You can make that whole walk indoors through the +15 skywalk, if you know the way. Plus 15 does.

• Search any building, shop or coffee spot downtown
• Get a route that names every bridge: “over 8 Ave SW into The CORE”
• Pick Mostly indoors on the coldest days
• Step-free routes, live +15 hours and bridge closures
• Coming from outside downtown? Your maps app takes you to the best +15 entrance, then Plus 15 guides you inside

109 buildings. 80 bridges. 16 km of heated walkways, 15 feet above the street. One clear indoor map of downtown Calgary.

No account. No ads. No tracking.
