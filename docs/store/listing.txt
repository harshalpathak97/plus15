SHORT:
Walk Calgary's +15 skywalks with ease: indoor routes, 141 places, live hours.
FULL:
Calgary's +15 is 16 km of heated indoor bridges linking more than 100 downtown buildings, 15 feet above the street. It's brilliant in January and baffling the first time you use it. Plus 15 makes it easy.

FIND YOUR WAY INDOORS
• Routes through the +15 between any two buildings, or from where you are right now
• Step-by-step directions by named bridges and buildings, so you're never guessing which door to take
• Live guidance that follows your walk, with a clear "You've arrived" at the end
• Step-free routes that avoid stairs-only links, and a Mostly indoors option for the coldest days
• Honest walk times, and outdoor sections clearly marked when a route has to leave the +15

KNOW WHAT'S OPEN
• +15 network hours at a glance, including weekends and holidays
• Published bridge closures, avoided automatically
• 141 shops, restaurants, coffee spots and services with real brand logos and open-now status

ASK +15
Ask in plain words: "coffee near Bankers Hall", "food courts open now", "step-free route to City Hall". Answers use the app's own buildings, shops and route planner, with one tap to start walking. Optional, and only used when you ask.

MADE FOR EVERYONE
• Light and dark mode
• Works with large text and screen readers
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Your precise location never leaves your phone

See the network in 3D, save the routes you walk every day, and start them in one tap.
