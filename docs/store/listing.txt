Calgary +15 skywalk map with indoor walking routes, shops and open hours
FULL:
It’s −30°. You’re in Bankers Hall. Your meeting is in The Bow.

You can make that whole walk indoors through the Calgary +15 skywalk, if you know the way. Plus 15 does.

• Search any building, shop or coffee spot in downtown Calgary
• Get an indoor route that names every bridge: “over 8 Ave SW into The CORE”
• Pick Mostly indoors on the coldest days
• Step-free routes, +15 hours and bridge closures
• Coming from outside downtown? Your maps app takes you to the best +15 entrance, then Plus 15 guides you inside

109 buildings. 80 bridges. 130+ shops, food courts and services. 16 km of heated walkways, 15 feet above the street. One clear Plus 15 map of downtown Calgary.

WHO IT’S FOR
Downtown office workers who want to get to a meeting, the gym or lunch without a coat. Visitors and conference-goers at the TELUS Convention Centre and the downtown hotels. Newcomers who keep coming out of the wrong door. Anyone downtown when it’s cold, windy or icy.

HOW IT WORKS
Pick where you are and where you’re going. Plus 15 finds the walkway route and tells you which bridge to cross, which floor to take and where the route goes outside, if it has to. Live guidance follows you bridge by bridge, and a 3D view shows how the buildings connect. Save routes you take every day. The +15 map and routing keep working when your signal drops deep inside a building.

QUESTIONS PEOPLE ASK

What is the Calgary +15?
The +15 (Plus 15) is one of the world’s largest pedestrian skywalk systems: enclosed bridges about 15 feet above the street that link most downtown office towers, malls and hotels.

When is the +15 open?
The City lists 6 am to 9 pm on weekdays and 9 am to 7 pm on weekends and holidays, and the walkways are closed on December 25. Some buildings and bridges keep shorter hours, and Plus 15 shows the ones we know about.

How do I get into the +15 from the street?
Most towers have an escalator or elevator up to the +15 level near the main doors. Tap Find nearest +15 entrance and Plus 15 walks you there along the sidewalk.

Is there a Plus 15 map I can use on my phone?
Yes, that’s this app. It also lists shops and food courts along the walkway, with opening hours.
